NESTED SCALAR/DRIFT AND QUADRUPOLE DIAGNOSTICS
Research continuation, 2026-10-21 UTC

1. Question and scope
---------------------
The October 20 free-bath continuation reports a six-moment approximation
for scattering of two hypothetical scalar species against an ideal bath.
Can additional radial functions and a new angular sector reveal slower
retained relaxation? This calculation answers that limited question.

It retains the fixed reference model:
  scalar masses m1=10, m2=11.5 GeV; Higgs mass mh=125 GeV;
  K11=K22=+0.001, K12=-0.001 (only Kij squared enters here);
  ideal Maxwell-Boltzmann leptons e, mu, tau and free quarks u,d,s,c,b;
  quark masses 0.002,0.005,0.100,1.300,4.200 GeV, respectively;
  two bath spins and both charges, with quark color multiplicity three;
  fixed g*=60 and non-reduced Planck mass 1.2209e19 GeV.

H(T)=sqrt(8*pi^3*60/90)*T^2/(1.2209e19 GeV).

All singlet dispersions and matrix elements use the inherited vacuum
tree model. The bath is held fixed. Scalar self-collisions, number-changing
channels, decay redistribution, interacting QCD, and time-dependent
background evolution are not included. The declared interval is
1/3 <= T <= 0.5 GeV. The retained tree scattering amplitude is

  |M_ij|^2 = Kij^2 * mf^2 * (4*mf^2 - t)/(mh^2 - t)^2.

2. Trial spaces and independent integration
-------------------------------------------
For species i define x_i=(E_i-m_i)/T. The trial functions are:

  scalar:     1, x_i, x_i^2
  drift:      p_z/T, x_i*p_z/T
  quadrupole: (p_z^2-|p|^2/3)/T^2

These give six scalar functions, four drift representatives, and two
quadrupole representatives. Isotropy supplies equal rates for the other
angular components; counting all those components would give dimension28.
The old centered-energy functions span exactly the same subspace as
1,x_i. Basis centering therefore does not explain the changed eigenvalues.

The equilibrium metric retains the actual relative species weights.
The only exact collision invariant projected out is common total scalar
number. Energy and drift relax against the fixed bath; they are not
projected out as conserved quantities.

The new implementation evaluates explicit Lorentz boosts and angular
averages. The inherited implementation uses analytic Bessel reductions.
Both share the same physical amplitude and assumptions, but their
integrations of the original six-moment matrix are independent.

The boost integration uses v=(q/T)(cosh(eta)-1) and generalized
Gauss-Laguerre quadrature with index 1/2. Invariant-energy integration
uses the appropriate elastic or conversion threshold power. Scattering
angles use Gauss-Legendre quadrature; boost directions combine polar
Gauss-Legendre and azimuthal trapezoidal rules. A final common spatial
rotation is averaged by exact scalar invariants.

One useful angular identity is
  <Q(p)Q(p')> = (2/15)*[(p.p')^2 - |p|^2*|p'|^2/3],
where Q(p)=p_z^2-|p|^2/3. Elastic channels have their reversible-form
factor 1/2; each paired conversion contribution is included once.

The generalized eigenproblem is D*c=lambda*G*c. Cholesky whitening of
the positive metric and projection of its transformed number-null
vector produce the reported positive trial-space rates.

4. Verification and its limits
------------------------------
At T=0.4, the entire six-moment collision matrix, transformed back to
the old centered-energy basis, agrees with the independently rerun
inherited matrix to relative Frobenius error 9.632e-14.
The independently integrated metric agrees to 1.442e-15.

The maximum relative change among reported eigenvalues between the
q48 and q80 setups is 2.11e-12. The exact common-number residual is zero
at reported precision; the symmetry residual is about 4.60e-18.
All nonconserved retained eigenvalues are positive. Scalar and drift
Cauchy interlacing checks pass. Boost-normalization checks agree with
the exact scaled-Bessel normalization to floating-point precision.

Endpoint full baseline-matrix errors are 7.139e-13 and 6.090e-13.
Endpoint nesting/interlacing checks pass. Multiple quadrature
refinements were performed at the central temperature; endpoint
results were checked against their independent original matrices.

These are numerical consistency checks. They do not measure physical
QCD uncertainty, omitted trial functions, or a rigorous rounding-error
interval. Internal review by separate AI agents is not independent
experimental reproduction or external scientific peer review.

5. What the inequalities mean
-----------------------------
For a fixed reversible nonnegative collision operator, with the exact
null direction removed, nested trial spaces obey for exact integrals

  lambda_full <= lambda_(N+1) <= lambda_N.

Thus enlarging the space can discover slower behavior. It cannot by
itself certify a positive lower bound on the full spectral gap. The
computed floating-point values are numerical estimates of those
variational upper bounds.

The new drift witness at T=0.4 gives an approximate upper witness
lambda_full/H <= 0.510586637. The scalar sector independently has the
upper witness 0.747848884. Neither comparison justifies a uniform
faster-than-expansion relaxation statement for this model.

Drift and quadrupole need not be populated in a homogeneous isotropic
cosmological preparation. The scalar result is the relevant witness
within that isotropic sector. An instantaneous comparison with H also
does not determine a time-dependent abundance or prove that a particular
initial state departs from its moving equilibrium distribution.

At T=0.5 all displayed positive rates exceed H. Because they are upper
bounds, that fact does not prove full equilibration. Higher radial
functions, other angular sectors, and high-energy tails remain open.
Quadrature convergence and interlacing do not close those omissions.

6. Next calculations
--------------------
A useful next step is momentum-resolved evolution in the same declared
free model, with momentum/tail sensitivity assessed on the observables
of interest. A claim about the full global gap would additionally need
control of the omitted space, for example a valid coercivity bound or
a tail analysis. No such positive lower-bound certificate is supplied.

Physical cosmological predictions require the interacting matched bath
response, number-changing collision network, thermodynamics, initial
conditions, and expansion history. These requirements are separate
from the numerical success of the present free-bath extension.
