1. Reversible radial cell method
--------------------------------
Let cell a specify both singlet species and an energy interval. Let pi_a
be its normalized equilibrium Maxwell-Boltzmann mass, retaining the true
relative species populations. Let G=diag(pi). For each equilibrium
scattering event with positive weight w, incoming cell a, and outgoing
cell b, add

  D += w (e_a-e_b)(e_a-e_b)^T.

The inherited elastic factor1/2 and conversion21-once convention must
already be included in w. If E_ab is the oriented event histogram, then
W=E+E^T and D=diag(W*1)-W implement exactly the expression above.
Self-cell events cancel. D is symmetric positive semidefinite, has
nonpositive off-diagonals, and D*1=0.

Relative occupations g=f/f_eq obey dg/dt=-G^(-1)Dg. Actual normalized
cell masses m=Gg obey dm/dt=-D G^(-1)m. Thus the mass generator has
nonnegative off-diagonals and zero column sums; pi is stationary. The
semigroup preserves total number and nonnegativity. The whitened
operator is A=G^(-1/2)DG^(-1/2), whose known null vector is sqrt(pi).
For tau=H*t, replace D by D/H. Finite positive eigenvalues are not a
positive lower bound on the full continuum gap.

A final interval [L,infinity) retains all events and equilibrium mass.
It is a lumped tail, not resolved asymptotics. Moving L and refining the
energy cells tests finite-time observable sensitivity to that choice.
No distribution beyond L should silently be discarded or reflected.

2. Initial conditions and fair observable comparison
----------------------------------------------------
The agreed zero-mean perturbations are

  hP = 1_{species2} - r2,
  hE = (E - Ebar_global)/T,
  g(0) = 1 + epsilon*h, epsilon=0.05.

Both are bounded below and give a nonnegative initial distribution for
these benchmark parameters. Bin initial values should be equilibrium
conditional averages of h. hP is exactly representable on every species
grid; hE is projected onto cell constants. Its discrete initial variance
is therefore slightly smaller than the continuum variance because
within-cell energy variance is omitted. Report that ratio or otherwise
distinguish own-initial-norm from common-continuum normalization when
comparing time correlations and L2 norms with polynomial moment models.

Because the bath is fixed and singlets are dilute classical particles,
the scattering equation is linear in their distribution. The cell
semigroup therefore evolves actual nonnegative cell populations. Fixed
T evolution has no Hubble redshift or moving bath and does not constitute
an expanding-universe or relic-abundance calculation. H is only the time
unit used here.

3. Quadrature and grid qualifications
-------------------------------------
Polynomial-moment quadrature convergence cannot be transferred to cell
indicator functions: cell boundaries introduce discontinuities. Refine
event-energy, boost, scattering-angle, and orientation quadratures as
well as the energy-cell grid. Shared event quadratures and nested cell
aggregation guarantee algebraic eigenvalue interlacing; that guarantee
does not prove convergence to the continuum integral.

An independently useful check is the exact count-only contrast, which
is representable by every species/bin grid. Conditional cell energy
projections should approach the inherited energy-moment matrix as cells
are refined. Conservation, detailed balance, and positivity are separate
structural checks.

4. Conditional zero-global-gap theorem
--------------------------------------
Assume the declared vacuum-tree, fixed ideal MB, scattering-only model
is extended to all singlet momenta. For every bath fermion f and scalar
transition i->j, physical t=(k-k')^2<=0 since the incoming/outgoing bath
masses agree. Write u=-t>=0. The amplitude is

  |M_ij|^2 = K_ij^2 mf^2 (4mf^2+u)/(mh^2+u)^2 <= A_ijf < infinity.

For the present bath masses mh^2>=8mf^2, its exact maximum is

  A_ijf = K_ij^2 mf^2 / [4(mh^2-4mf^2)],

attained at u=mh^2-8mf^2. (For mh^2<8mf^2 the maximum is at u=0.)

Using lambda_i=4s*p_i^2, the t interval of width4*p_i*p_f, and the
Moller relative velocity v_M=sqrt(s)*p_i/(E_i*epsilon_f),

  sigma_ij <= A_ijf*p_f/(16*pi*s*p_i),
  v_M*sigma_ij <= A_ijf*p_f/(16*pi*sqrt(s)*E_i*epsilon_f)
               <= A_ijf/(32*pi*E_i*epsilon_f).

The apparent exothermic threshold divergence cancels in v_M*sigma.
Summing channels and integrating the MB bath gives

  nu_i(p) <= B_i(T)/E_i(p),
  B_i(T) = sum_{f,j} g_f*A_ijf/(32*pi)
           * integral d^3k/(2*pi)^3 exp(-epsilon_f/T)/epsilon_f.

The bath integral is finite (equal to mf*T*K1(mf/T)/(2*pi^2)). Let D_R
contain both scalar species with E_i>R. Its equilibrium mass is positive
and tends to zero as R grows. For the centered indicator of D_R, the
reversible Dirichlet form equals outward equilibrium flux and the
variance is pi(D_R)*(1-pi(D_R)). Hence

  Rayleigh(1_{D_R}-pi(D_R))
    <= max_i B_i(T) / [R*(1-pi(D_R))] -> 0.

Indicators belong to the form domain because nu<=B/m_min is bounded.
Nonnegativity of the collision form then proves that this globally
extrapolated model has GLOBAL L2 spectral gap exactly zero.

This conclusion concerns uniform relaxation over arbitrarily rare,
arbitrarily energetic perturbations. It does not preclude rapid or
well-converged finite-time relaxation of thermally populated observables.
It does not apply without reanalysis after adding other collision
channels, changing the bath, or specifying a physical ultraviolet
completion of the vacuum-tree model. No large-momentum numerical claim
is required for the conditional analytic theorem.

5. Initial implementation audit
-------------------------------
Reviewed radial_free_bath.py initial draft. The following match the
independently verified inherited event measure:
  - exact cancellation of kve(1,z) between invariant-energy and boost
    weights;
  - final energy orientation cosine c*mu+sqrt(1-c^2)*sqrt(1-mu^2)*cos(phi);
  - one spin/charge multiplicity and one quark color multiplicity;
  - elastic1/2 and conversion21-once bookkeeping;
  - symmetric event-graph assembly and correct G^(-1)D orientation;
  - nested aggregation P^T D P with correspondingly aggregated metric.

The final implementation uses the agreed hP/hE definitions and epsilon.
It retains the null eigenvalue numerically rather than explicitly projecting
it; the final value is 7.11e-15 in units of H, well separated from the first
positive finite-graph eigenvalue near0.7391. Direct number, positivity, and
independent forward-exponential checks confirm that this roundoff-scale
null treatment does not affect the reported trajectories materially.

6. Polynomial-evolution comparison audit
----------------------------------------
Reviewed comparison/moment_evolution.py. With the raw interleaved basis
(1_1,1_2,x_1,x_2,x_1^2,x_2^2), the implemented population coefficients
(-r2,r1,0,0,0,0) and energy coefficients
(m1/T-Ebar/T,m2/T-Ebar/T,1,1,0,0) represent the agreed initial functions.
The mean Ebar/T is correctly evaluated as number^T G energy_over_T.

For G=L L^T, whitening z=L^T c gives A=L^(-1)D L^(-T). The code's two
triangular solves implement this transformation correctly. Q projects
orthogonally to L^T times the exact common-number coefficient vector.
The reduced matrix is divided by H once. If a_k are initial coefficients
in its orthonormal eigenbasis and lambda_k are its positive rates/H,
the code correctly evaluates

  C(tau)/C(0) = sum_k a_k^2 exp(-lambda_k*tau) / sum_k a_k^2,
  ||h(tau)||/||h(0)|| = sqrt(sum_k a_k^2 exp(-2*lambda_k*tau)
                                / sum_k a_k^2).

The four-function and six-function spaces have respectively three and
five nonconserved scalar modes. Drift is appropriately absent from this
isotropic comparison. The separate count-only population curve is the
single exponential with the inherited count-only rate.

The population correlation equals the remaining heavy-species excess
divided by its initial value. The energy correlation equals the remaining
mean-total-energy excess divided by its initial value for that initial
perturbation. This observable interpretation does not require interpreting
the smallest finite-matrix eigenvalue as a continuum spectral gap.

7. Final postprocessor and numerical audit
------------------------------------------
Reviewed analyze_convergence.py, final_evolution.json, and the relevant
quantities in convergence_summary.json. The raw finest event graph uses
192 finite cells per species with width0.125 through x=24, followed by a
semi-infinite cell. The presented result aggregates it to128 finite cells
through x=16 followed by a semi-infinite cell. P^T D P and P^T pi retain
all discarded-as-individual cells inside the aggregate tail; no mass or
events are removed. The tail-start checks at12,16,20,24 all use that same
finest event graph. Old quadrature runs are postprocessed with the same
frozen initial-condition definitions and time grid.

For the final graph, equilibrium species fractions are approximately
(0.972071507,0.027928493), tail mass is9.4504e-7, the count-only diagnostic
is2.97474576498998 H, and the number-null relative matrix residual is

At tau=1,2,5 the normalized population-excess correlations are

The separate review/verify_final.py constructs the forward mass generator

No concrete normalization, generator-orientation, initial-condition,
