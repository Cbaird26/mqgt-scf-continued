EXPANDING FREE-BATH SCATTERING MODULE

Scope
-----
The temperature window is T0=0.5 GeV to Tf=1/3 GeV, inherited from the
declared free-bath source interval. The radiation history is prescribed:
g*=g*s=60, T*a=T0*a0, and H(T)=sqrt(8*pi^3*60/90)*T^2/M_Pl.
The initial distributions are zero-chemical-potential Maxwell-Boltzmann
populations at T0. The two masses are 10 and 11.5 GeV. The included
scattering amplitudes, fermion masses, bath multiplicities, elastic 1/2,
and paired conversion counting are inherited from the previous checked
radial_free_bath.py implementation. No timelike width enters scattering.

Comoving coordinates and closure
-------------------------------
Use y=p/T and eta=ln(T0/T)=ln(a/a0). A fixed y cell follows exact
collisionless redshift when T*a is constant. Its comoving population
M_a=a^3*n_a/n_eq(T0) is therefore constant in the absence of collisions.
The implementation introduces no finite-difference advection operator.

The event quadrature is routed onto 192 equal y cells from 0 through 48,
of width 0.25, followed by a semi-infinite [48,infinity) cell for each
species. Every quadrature event is assigned to a cell. No finite tail is
discarded or reflected. Nested coarser grids and shorter tail starts use
unions of cells and their conductances.

The finite-volume closure reconstructs the distribution inside each cell
as a multiple of the instantaneous equilibrium shape. It preserves the
exact cell population under collisionless expansion, but within-cell
energy/pressure reconstruction is approximate. The supplied independent
continuum collisionless integral quantifies that observable error; finer
momentum cells reduce it. At 192 cells, its final collisionless Tkin/T
error is approximately 0.0127% relatively.

Positive collision generator
----------------------------
At each bank temperature, positive equilibrium event weights yield a
symmetric off-diagonal conductance W. Its Dirichlet matrix is
D=diag(W*1)-W. Store W/H(T), where the event normalization is total
n_eq(T). Interpolate these nonnegative conductances linearly in eta.
Recompute the exact instantaneous equilibrium cell probabilities pi(T)
at every evolution midpoint. Then the comoving-population generator is

  Q = [W - diag(W*1)] * diag(pi)^(-1),
  dM/deta = Q M,

where W already includes division by H. Q has nonnegative off-diagonal
entries, zero column sums, and Q*pi=0. The scattering-only evolution uses
the exponential of the midpoint generator, evaluated by expm_multiply.
No clipping or population renormalization is applied.

The final bank has 16 regular eta intervals plus the T=0.4 GeV snapshot
node (18 temperature nodes total). It reuses the ten original q48 bank
graphs exactly and adds only the eight missing midpoint temperatures.
The final event quadrature orders are (q,boost,scattering,direction,azimuth)
=(48,32,24,24,32). The supplied q40 bank provides the final matched
quadrature control; the historical q24 pilot is not included.
All quadrature comparisons should use the common eight-interval bank;
temperature refinement is assessed separately at fixed quadrature.

Observables
-----------
The reported kinetic temperature is Tkin=<p^2/(3E)> for the actual mixture.
It equals T exactly for Maxwell-Boltzmann momentum shapes at temperature
T, independently of species fractions. The module also reports the heavy
fraction, mean total and kinetic energy, physical number density, and
total-variation distances from (a) the common-fugacity equilibrium mixture
and (b) Maxwell-Boltzmann momentum shapes with the actual species fractions.
The latter separates momentum-shape departures from population changes.

For number-changing integration, M_eq=a^3*n_eq(T)*pi(T)/n_eq(T0), not pi
alone. This module exposes neq(T), cell_data(T,edges), ScatteringBank,
and observables for the separately implemented combined reaction network.

Reproduction commands
---------------------
The package-level reproduce_all.py is the recommended entry point. For
manual bank reconstruction, run from this directory and set
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 for predictable timing.

python expanding_free_bath.py bank --output bank_q40.npz --bins 192 --ymax 48 --intervals 8 --q 40 --boost 28 --scattering 20 --direction 20 --azimuth 32
python expanding_free_bath.py bank --output bank_q48_eta16.npz --bins 192 --ymax 48 --intervals 16 --q 48 --boost 32 --scattering 24 --direction 24 --azimuth 32
python expanding_free_bath.py evolve --bank bank_q48_eta16.npz --output scattering_final.json --steps 192

analyze_scattering.py can independently regenerate the isolated scattering
refinements from these supplied banks. The final combined-network study
has its own matched refinements. Older pilot output files are omitted
from the deliverable because they are not the final numerical result.

All reported refinement differences are empirical sensitivities. They
are not certified error bounds or physical-QCD uncertainty estimates.
