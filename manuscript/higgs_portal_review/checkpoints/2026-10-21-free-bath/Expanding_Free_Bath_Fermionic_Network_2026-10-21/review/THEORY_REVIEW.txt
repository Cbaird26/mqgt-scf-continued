1. Scope and clock
------------------
Use the inherited two-scalar masses and portal vertices, the declared ideal
Maxwell-Boltzmann fermion bath, and constant g*=g*s=60. The primary diagnostic
window is T0=0.5 GeV to Tf=1/3 GeV, with T=0.4 GeV retained as a snapshot.
The initial zero-chemical-potential MB distribution is a specified initial
preparation. It is not evidence that the reaction network dynamically
established chemical equilibrium before the start.

For constant entropy degrees of freedom, a=T0/T and eta=ln(a). With y=p/T,
free streaming preserves y. The homogeneous equation in these coordinates is

  partial_eta f_i(y,eta) = collision_i[f;T]/H(T),

where collision_i is defined per unit bath-frame time. If a Lorentz-invariant
collision term C_i is used instead, collision_i=C_i/E_i. These conventions
must not be mixed.

2. Comoving cell masses and scattering
--------------------------------------
Let n0 be the total physical equilibrium scalar density at T0, a0=1.
Define M_a=a^3*n_a/n0 for a fixed y-cell a. In a collisionless run every M_a
is constant. This is the exact cell-mass statement of cosmological redshift.
Physical number density is n0*a^-3*sum_a M_a.

The instantaneous equilibrium physical cell densities n_eq,a(T) determine
both the normalized shape pi_a(T)=n_eq,a/n_eq,total and the absolute reference
M_eq,a=a^3*n_eq,a/n0. Their distinction is essential for annihilation.

If D(T) is the inherited equilibrium scattering Dirichlet matrix divided
by n_eq,total, the forward mass generator in eta is

  Q(T) = -D(T)*diag(pi(T))^(-1)/H(T).

Its off-diagonal entries are nonnegative, its column sums vanish, and
Q*pi=0. The scattering equation dM/deta=Q*M preserves total comoving
number. Decay/inverse-decay transitions with one scalar on either side
can be added to the same reversible construction.

Evolving M avoids an extra moving-reference term. If relative occupations
g=M/pi(T) are evolved instead, their derivative must include
-g*partial_eta ln(pi). Simply reusing the fixed-temperature equation for g
would incorrectly hold the reference equilibrium fixed.

A safe temperature interpolation linearly interpolates nonnegative
conductances in D/H, reconstructs the diagonal from their row sums, and
uses pi(T) integrated at the current temperature. This preserves the
reversible graph structure and instantaneous equilibrium exactly at the
matrix level. Interpolation is still a numerical approximation and needs
its own temperature-node refinement check.

3. Pair annihilation and inverse production
-------------------------------------------
The interaction is L_int=-(v/2)*h*sum_ij K_ij*phi_i*phi_j, with symmetric
K and vertex -i*v*K_ij. Its spin-averaged fermion scattering amplitude
crosses to the spin/color-summed annihilation amplitude

  |M_ij->f fbar|^2 =
    2*Nc*K_ij^2*mf^2*(s-4mf^2) / [(s-mh^2)^2+mh^2*Gamma_h^2].

There is no factor 4 for fermion bath spin/charge in this annihilation
amplitude: the final spins are already summed, the final f and fbar are
one distinct pair, and color is counted once.

For isotropic final angles and scalar initial states,

  sigma_ijf(s) = Nc*K_ij^2*mf^2*(s-4mf^2)^(3/2)
    / [8*pi*sqrt(s)*sqrt(lambda(s,mi^2,mj^2))*Den(s)],

where Den is the propagator denominator above. Multiplication by the
Moller velocity sqrt(lambda)/(2*E_i*E_j) gives the smooth threshold kernel

  sigma*v_M = Nc*K_ij^2*mf^2*(s-4mf^2)^(3/2)
    / [16*pi*sqrt(s)*E_i*E_j*Den(s)].

This must be set to zero below final threshold. At equal initial masses
and in their center-of-momentum frame, it reduces to
Nc*K^2*mf^2*beta_f^3/[4*pi*Den], a useful normalization check.

Let A_ab(T) be the equilibrium-conditional average of sigma*v_M over
initial momentum cells a,b and relative orientation (1/2 integral dc).
It is symmetric under exchanging a and b. Classical detailed balance gives
the cell equation

  dM_a/deta|ann = (n0*a^-3/H) sum_b A_ab
                   * [M_eq,a*M_eq,b - M_a*M_b].

The inverse source therefore uses the absolute M_eq, not pi alone. At
fixed T this term vanishes exactly on M=M_eq. At M_a=0 it is nonnegative,
so the continuous finite-cell system is quasipositive. Summing cell masses
recovers the usual number-density annihilation/inverse equation for
thermal shapes.

No extra 1/2 belongs in this per-particle loss equation for identical
initial scalars or a=b. Identical-pair event counting contains 1/2 but
each event removes two scalars. For distinct cells a,b, the reaction
contributes once to the derivative of each cell, again removing two total.
An unordered-pair implementation must implement this stoichiometry
explicitly; an ordered full matrix implementation already includes it.

4. Timelike pole and the new width input
----------------------------------------
The zero-width spacelike propagator cannot be used globally after crossing:
the s-channel pole at s=mh^2 makes its exact all-momentum thermal integral
divergent, even at temperatures where the resonant population is tiny.
Missing the pole with finite quadrature does not resolve that issue.

The parent calculation has explicitly chosen Gamma_h=0.0041 GeV as an
additional benchmark input, sourced to the PDG 2025 Higgs review. The
fixed-width resonance prescription must be declared in the new model
metadata. No separate on-shell Higgs population/process should be added
to the same inclusive ffbar annihilation kernel without subtraction of
real intermediate states. A width sensitivity or a conservative resonant
tail estimate is appropriate if the chosen thermal window makes that
assumption immaterial in practice.

5. Collisionless and equilibrium observable controls
----------------------------------------------------
The comoving collisionless initial distribution is exactly

  f0_i(y)=exp[-sqrt(mi^2+T0^2*y^2)/T0].

At a later T, integrate this same f0(y) against sqrt(mi^2+T^2*y^2) for
mean energy and against T^2*y^2/[3*sqrt(mi^2+T^2*y^2)] for pressure per
particle. These direct continuum integrals independently test redshift.

Define Tkin=<p^2/(3E)> over the actual distribution. Any relativistic
classical MB distribution at temperature T has Tkin=T, by integration
by parts. This identity holds for a mixture too. It gives a clean kinetic
equilibrium diagnostic. Mean (E-m)/T has additional relativistic corrections.

Cell masses alone do not determine arbitrary within-cell observables.
Using the current-equilibrium conditional shape within a cell is a
declared closure. It causes a finite-cell error even in a collisionless
run, because the actual collisionless shape retains f0(y). Compare to
the direct continuum control and show grid convergence rather than
calling this conditional reconstruction exact free streaming.

6. Required numerical evidence
------------------------------
Verify positive cell masses, comoving-number conservation for scattering
and decay, instantaneous equilibrium cancellation of each kernel, and
agreement of pair-loss normalization with an independent thermal integral.
Compare grid, collision quadrature, temperature-table interpolation, and
time integration refinements only to the accuracy needed for the stated
observables. Changing total abundance requires a declared n0; normalized
shape evolution alone cannot predict annihilation.

The previous conditional zero-gap theorem applies to the previous
globally extended scattering-only fixed-bath operator. It is not a global
gap theorem for this time-dependent nonlinear reaction network.

The tiny annihilation effect is conditional on the initial density
n0=1.7005907435801984e-9 GeV^3. Equilibrium annihilation loss/H is already

scalar reactions remain scope limitations, separately from numerical
resolution. The former fixed-temperature zero-gap argument is not a
