# Two-singlet EFT and experimental interfaces — public technical review

**Status:** Author-approved public *draft* on a review branch; NOT peer reviewed, not journal-submitted, not a GitHub release, not a DOI mint, and not an experimental finding. The PR is unmerged; frozen independent-verification release and original corpus are unchanged.

This package develops a restricted symmetric-vacuum, two-real-singlet effective theory motivated by the MQGT-SCF source archive, plus conditional open-system and H2 interfaces. It does not verify MQGT-SCF as a Theory of Everything or establish any consciousness/ethical scalar interpretation.

- `COMPREHENSIVE_WORKING_PAPER_2026-09-21.md`: broad dated synthesis of exploratory Research Notes 01–13; derived and illustrative results distinguished from unresolved links.
- `DUAL_SINGLET_HIGGS_PORTAL_DRAFT.md`: narrower dated collider working paper.
- `CLAIMS_AND_RELEASE_GATE.md`: evidence/assumption ledger and release requirements; read with later addenda, not as an automatically updated manuscript.
- `RESEARCH_NOTE_14_DECAY_AUDIT.md` through `RESEARCH_NOTE_17_THRESHOLD_AND_SPECTRAL_CERTIFICATION.md`: toy-model lepton and spectral checks, missing-channel inventory and hadronic-threshold caveats.
- `RESEARCH_NOTE_18_PARTIAL_SPECTRAL_MATCHING.md` through `RESEARCH_NOTE_21_SPECTRAL_SENSITIVITY_AND_OFFLINE_DATA_GATE.md`: paper-to-code matching for candidate low-energy pion/kaon amplitudes, upstream input provenance gate and a synthetic spectral-sensitivity calculation. No actual source Omnès inputs were obtained or parsed.
- `RESEARCH_NOTE_22_COMPRESSED_SPECTRAL_WINDOW_BENCHMARK.md`: *distinct* illustrative `m1=10,m2=11.5 GeV` point whose virtual-mass endpoint is 1.5 GeV, conditionally removing the earlier 2–15 GeV pion/kaon extrapolation. Its leptonic-only `c*tau <= ~645 km` is not a predicted lifetime.
- `RESEARCH_NOTE_23_COMPRESSED_SPECTRUM_ROBUSTNESS.md`: exact 2×2 mass/portal rotation, asymmetric bare-mass perturbation interval for a *provisional* `Delta<=2 GeV` endpoint, and algebraic additional-width ratios required for selected proper decay lengths. `spectrum_robustness.py` provides the conditional formulae. This is not a radiative calculation or detector constraint.
- `RESEARCH_NOTE_24_STABLE_PAIR_AND_INVISIBLE_ENVELOPE.md`: fixed stable-light-pair Higgs production under an exact common Z2, and an **idealized, assumption-dependent** invisible-event branching envelope of `0.1154091%–0.4606573%` for the Note-22 benchmark if the only Higgs widths are SM plus the three singlet-pair channels. `invisible_channel_envelope.py` and `test_research_note_24.py` provide the calculation and 12 locally passing algebra/regression tests. The two unknown heavy-state event-classification probabilities are **not detector acceptances**.
- `RESEARCH_NOTE_25_Z2_PHOTON_SELECTION_AND_H2_MATCHING_GATE.md`: an exact, branch-conditional common-Z2 selection rule excludes **single-odd-singlet production from a photon-only initial state** in the symmetric vacuum, including in an ordinary parity-even electromagnetic background. Formal Higgs exchange with a symbolic h-gamma-gamma coefficient instead produces **even** `s_i s_j F²` contact operators, including a checked factor-of-two for `s1 s2 F²`. `photon_selection_rules.py` and `test_research_note_25.py` provide 11 locally passing **algebra-only** checks; no photon rate, H2 coefficient, or collider constraint is calculated.
- `RESEARCH_NOTE_26_OPTICAL_PAIR_THRESHOLDS_AND_VACUUM_RESPONSE.md`: separates pair-production symmetry from **20/21.5/23-GeV** on-shell thresholds at the compressed point. An illustrative two-2-eV-photon vacuum collision cannot create those on-shell pairs; a constant Maxwell kinetic rescaling alone predicts no H2 visibility change. No detector-specific optical amplitude or hardware characterization is calculated.
- `RESEARCH_NOTE_27_STABLE_SINGLET_NUCLEON_SCATTERING.md`: a separate leading **elastic** Higgs-mediated nucleon-scattering calculation for stable `s1`, with `sigma_SI ≈ 7.40e-47 cm²` only at the illustrative `K11=.001, f_N=.30, m1=10 GeV` point. `stable_singlet_direct_detection.py` and `test_research_note_27.py` provide a conditional collider-width/scattering crossing identity and 12 locally passing algebra/regression tests. The local dark-matter density fraction, relic abundance, current direct-detection limits and real event rates were **not** computed.
- `RESEARCH_NOTE_28_THERMAL_POPULATION_AND_COANNIHILATION_GATE.md`: leptonic-only annihilation benchmark, conditional *nonrelativistic* heavy equilibrium fraction and why no abundance follows without complete conversion rates. `thermal_population_gate.py` and `test_research_note_28.py` provide 13 local mathematical tests.
- `RESEARCH_NOTE_29_THERMAL_CONVERSION_VERSUS_EXPANSION.md`: compares **only the known leptonic partial decay/inverse decay** with an illustrative radiation-era Hubble rate: `Gamma_l/H≈0.00116` at *assumed* `T=.5 GeV, g*=60`. Correcting the Note-28 ideal-gas nonrelativistic heavy fraction `5.7847%` to full Maxwell–Boltzmann gives `5.7215%` under the same conditional equilibrium assumption. Neither result establishes actual equilibrium, relic density, BBN safety or the total `s2` width. `thermal_conversion_gate.py` and `test_research_note_29.py` provide 16 locally passing math tests and a separate SciPy numerical cross-check.

Older test scripts cover the corresponding earlier toy algebra and synthetic fixtures. No external QCD code or numerical data were run or redistributed. The pinned four Omnès inputs remain unavailable; until authenticated and safely parsed, no pion/kaon width, total lifetime or full detector classification is established. An H2 visibility coefficient remains an instrument-level estimand pending a microscopic, detector-specific derivation.
