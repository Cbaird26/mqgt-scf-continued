A Theory of Everything - Expanded Research Collection (20 October 2026)

This archival research collection brings together the Baird / ZoraASI corpus and Jennifer Lorraine Nielsen's separately authored gauge-gravity preprint, with recent research papers, computational records, reproducibility audits and supporting materials. The public reading edition contains 9,252 pages: four new editorial pages, the reviewed 7,399-page public baseline, and a 1,849-page research supplement. The supplement contains 550 byte-distinct existing PDFs dated 19 September through 20 October 2026, plus a verbatim PDF rendering of the 20 October free-bath reference report. Identical file copies were removed; historical repetitions and distinct revisions may remain.

The archive supplies original supplemental PDFs, available authoring sources, the current report's code and numerical results, a detailed catalogue, physical page maps, SHA-256 checksums and assembly/verification tools. Its scope is the locally identified baseline and the documented research folders, not every cloud draft or repository.

Nielsen's included 116-page preprint is The Complex Hopf Fibration as the Canonical Space for Gauge-Gravity Unification: The Field, Universal Action, and Particle Spectrum, Preprints.org v5 (17 July 2026), DOI 10.20944/preprints202604.0315.v5, CC BY 4.0. Her authorship and notices are retained. Her 240-page manuscript without a stated reuse license and the personal study guide are not included in this public deposit. Inclusion does not assert her coauthorship of the Baird corpus or endorsement of the compilation.

This is a research archive, not a declaration of a completed or experimentally validated Theory of Everything. Claims retain their source statuses, assumptions and corrections.
