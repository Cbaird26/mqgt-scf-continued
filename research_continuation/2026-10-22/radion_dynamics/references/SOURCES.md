# Primary sources and how they are used

Checked on 22 October 2026. The following supply established mathematical background, not independent validation of the MQGT-SCF fixtures. No external paper is redistributed in this bundle.

- M. B. Law, I. M. Lopez and D. Santiago, **Positive mass and Dirac operators on weighted manifolds and smooth metric measure spaces**, [arXiv:2312.15441](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.15441), [HTML, Theorem 3.5](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.15441v2). The even-base warped Dirac formula supplies the fibre-volume connection term. Its specialization to the declared round monopole family is derived in the note.
- D. V. Vassilevich, **Heat kernel expansion: user's manual**, Physics Reports 388 (2003) 279-360, [arXiv:hep-th/0306138](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0306138). The Dirac endomorphism and heat coefficient fix the external curvature sign and normalization.
- D. I. Kaiser, **Conformal transformations with multiple scalar fields**, Physical Review D 81 (2010) 084044, [arXiv:1003.1159](https://arxiv.org/abs/1003.1159), equations (29)-(32). The note applies the Weyl transformation to a general pre-existing Jordan kinetic metric and derives the vacuum-normalized generalized mass problem.
- B. Iochum, C. Levy and D. Vassilevich, **Spectral action beyond the weak-field approximation**, Communications in Mathematical Physics 316 (2012) 595-613, [arXiv:1108.3749](https://arxiv.org/abs/1108.3749). Background for the heat/Duhamel expansion and the distinction between a finite derivative truncation and a full momentum-dependent spectral action.
