REACTION-NETWORK AUDIT FOR THE EXPANDING FREE-BATH BENCHMARK
2026-10-21

1. Model lineage and scope

The inherited files are leptonic_conversion_scattering.py and Research
Note 52 at repository commit
994e51cb28b51ade500bb0e1902f3b082ef8e594, under
https://github.com/Cbaird26/mqgt-scf-continued/tree/994e51cb28b51ade500bb0e1902f3b082ef8e594/manuscript/higgs_portal_review

The source explicitly declares the off-diagonal interaction
L_int = -v*K12*h*phi1*phi2 -(mf/v)*h*fbar*f. The symmetric-matrix convention
for both diagonal and off-diagonal entries is

  L_int = -(v/2)*h*sum_ij Kij*phi_i*phi_j -(mf/v)*h*fbar*f,
  vertex(h phi_i phi_j) = -i*v*Kij.

The numerical benchmark has m1=10, m2=11.5, mh=125 GeV,
K11=K22=+0.001 and K12=-0.001. The bath fermions are e,mu,tau,u,d,s,c,b,
with the same fixed masses, ideal MB occupations, and color factors used
in the preceding free-bath calculation. The cosmological extension uses
T0=0.5 to Tf=1/3 GeV, within the inherited temperature interval, and an
initial scalar MB distribution with zero chemical potential. Its absolute
density is consequently fixed, rather than chosen to enlarge reactions.

The evolved network consists of the leading-portal-order fermionic channels:
  phi_i f <-> phi_j f and the charge-conjugate scattering channels;
  phi_i phi_j <-> f fbar, for ij=11,12,22;
  phi2 <-> phi1 f fbar, for each kinematically allowed flavor.

The last process changes species but preserves total scalar count. Only
annihilation/inverse pair production changes the total number in this list.
This is an explicitly restricted vacuum/free-fermion model, not a complete
interacting-QCD network or an empirically established particle model.

2. Crossing and identical-particle factors

The inherited scattering amplitude is averaged over the initial fermion
spin. Its factor 4 for an ideal bath counts two spins and both charges.
For scalar pair annihilation there is no initial spin average; the final
spin/color sum gives

  |M_ij->f fbar|^2 = 2*Nc*Kij^2*mf^2*(s-4mf^2)/D_h(s),
  D_h(s)=(s-mh^2)^2+(mh*Gamma_h)^2.

Therefore, with lambda_ij=lambda(s,mi^2,mj^2),

  sigma_ij = Nc*Kij^2*mf^2*(s-4mf^2)^(3/2)
             /[8*pi*sqrt(s)*sqrt(lambda_ij)*D_h(s)],

and in the bath frame

  sigma*v_M = Nc*Kij^2*mf^2*(s-4mf^2)^(3/2)
              /[16*pi*sqrt(s)*Ei*Ej*D_h(s)].

No additional bath spin/charge factor 4 multiplies these expressions.
There is no identical-initial-state factor in the tagged-particle loss
term. An unordered pair-event density has factor 1/(1+delta_ij); its
stoichiometric loss of two identical particles cancels that factor for
the single-species number equation. For i!=j each reaction removes one
particle from each species. Root's cell kernel uses a symmetric angular
average of sigma*v_M, with inverse production formed from the same kernel
and equilibrium cell populations. This enforces MB detailed balance.

The independent invariant thermal contraction is

  gamma_ij_ordered = T/(32*pi^4) integral ds
                    [lambda_ij*sigma_ij/sqrt(s)]*K1(sqrt(s)/T),
  <sigma*v_M>_ij = gamma_ij_ordered/(n_i_eq*n_j_eq).

After q=mi+mj+T*w, its scaled-Bessel prefactor is
1/[4*mi^2*mj^2*kve(2,mi/T)*kve(2,mj/T)], with threshold Boltzmann factors
retained if the final threshold exceeds the initial one. These rates
provide an independent check of the momentum-cell kernel contraction.

3. Timelike propagator input and remote tails

Crossing introduces a timelike Higgs pole. The unregularized tree
propagator is not integrable over an unbounded MB energy domain, even
when its pole has an extremely small thermal weight. The annihilation
calculation therefore explicitly adopts a constant Gamma_h=0.0041 GeV.
This is a new propagator prescription, supported as a nominal SM-width
scale by the PDG Higgs review; it was not needed or hidden in the prior
spacelike scattering calculation.

https://pdg.lbl.gov/2025/reviews/rpp2025-rev-higgs-boson.pdf

No separate on-shell Higgs population or Higgs-decay source is added to
the same annihilation term. Such an addition would require a consistent
real-intermediate-state treatment. The present low-temperature observable
support is far below the Higgs resonance.

reaction_rates.py integrates the thermal main region and bounds the
entire omitted region, including the resonance. For q>=qsplit,

  lambda*sigma <= Nc*K^2*mf^2*q^4/[8*pi*(mh*Gamma_h)^2].

Monotonicity of exp(q/T)*K1(q/T), combined with incomplete-Gamma moments
of q^4*exp[-(q-q0)/T], yields the saved upper bound. It is below
4.54e-24 relative to the main annihilation integral throughout the
inherited temperature window. This controls the omitted mathematical
tail of this prescription, not missing physical reactions.

Halving and doubling the width changes the bulk thermal rates by less
than 8.69e-10 and 3.48e-9 relatively, respectively. The portal vertices
would add an on-shell h->scalar width of about 1.9008e-5 GeV if that sector
were included in a total-width model (using explicitly declared v=246.22
GeV). Adding this amount shifts the low-temperature rates by about 1.08e-11.
These small sensitivities do not make the resonance prescription optional;
they show that the chosen low-temperature observables are insensitive to it.

4. Decay and inverse absorption with momentum redistribution

For z=m_ffbar^2, exact three-body phase space and the final fermion spin
sum give

  dGamma_2->1ff/dz = Nc*K12^2*mf^2*sqrt(lambda(M2^2,M1^2,z))
                    *(z-4mf^2)^(3/2)
                    /[128*pi^3*M2^3*sqrt(z)*(mh^2-z)^2].

The allowed interval is 4mf^2<z<(M2-M1)^2. Only e,mu,u,d,s are open.
This vacuum decay lies far below the Higgs pole; the inherited exact
massive denominator is retained. Computed partial widths, in GeV, are:

  e:   7.70506231008e-27
  mu:  3.06019064038e-22
  u:   3.54084207193e-25
  d:   2.21271642215e-24
  s:   8.28674363714e-22
  total: 1.13726793344e-21.

The e+mu sum is 3.06026769100e-22 GeV, agreeing with the inherited partial
width 3.060267688e-22 GeV. The quark contribution remains an ideal free-quark
diagnostic and must not be described as a physical hadronic decay width.

The graph integrates equilibrium parent momentum, time dilation M2/E2,
the differential z spectrum, and the daughter momentum after boosting
to the bath frame. Since the parent is spin 0 and the integrated spectrum
depends only on z, the rest-frame daughter direction is isotropic.
The bath-frame daughter energy is linear in cos(theta); daughter-cell
probabilities are integrated analytically as differences of a clipped
uniform-cosine CDF. The parent and daughter semi-infinite cells are retained.

Each forward equilibrium decay weight adds one symmetric conductance
between its species2 parent cell and species1 daughter cell. The inverse
phi1+f+fbar->phi2 process is supplied by MB detailed balance using this
same conductance. There is no extra factor 1/2 and no additional inverse
rate to add. The resulting graph preserves total scalar number and
includes physical momentum redistribution, rather than a scalar lifetime
inserted into a fictitious kinetic-relaxation entry.

decay_conductance(T,edges) returns W normalized by total N_eq(T), in GeV.
It has not been divided by H. The expanding eta generator uses W/H.
Both decay_bank.npz and decay_bank_eta16.npz store keys edges,eta,
conductances and an explicit units string.

The graph's integrated forward rate agrees with the independent
r2_eq*Gamma_total*K1(M2/T)/K2(M2/T) contraction to better than 9e-14 across
the temperature nodes. At T=.4, doubling parent/spectral orders changes
W by 1.1647e-4 and D by 3.04195e-5 in relative Frobenius norm. Smooth energy
and population-energy test forms change by approximately 6.4e-8 and 2.3e-8.
These are empirical quadrature sensitivities, not certified error bounds.

5. Rate scales and omitted scalar self-collision sector

At temperatures (1/3,.4,.5)GeV, the equilibrium annihilation loss rate
N_eq*<sigma_eff*v>/H is approximately
(5.273e-9,7.306e-7,1.012e-4). The linearized common-fugacity chemical
relaxation diagnostic is twice those values. These rates depend on the
absolute scalar density. They are distinct from the earlier scattering
operator, whose normalized rates were independent of common fugacity.

At the same temperatures, the MB decay/inverse-decay species-contrast
rates are approximately(0.00944,0.00660,0.00430)H. Decay is included in
the evolution; its effect cannot be inferred solely from a lifetime.
The rate survey at .6,.8,1GeV is exploratory outside the inherited
temperature interval and is not a cosmological freeze-out computation.

The portal vertices also mediate scalar-only 2->2 scattering through
s,t,u Higgs exchange, at O(K^4) in the cross section. Their minimal
amplitude is fixed by K and v, while independent scalar contact quartics
or cubic interactions require additional numerical inputs. Those values
are not supplied by the inherited scattering benchmark, which explicitly
excluded self-interactions. Their absence from this leading-order
fermionic evolution must not be interpreted as a complete-sector result.

scalar_exchange_audit.py evaluates the known Higgs-exchange contribution
for all initial/final pairs 11,12,22. At T=.5 its equilibrium tagged-particle
total rates are 1.23766e-5H and 3.22551e-5H for species 1 and 2; the equilibrium
average is 1.35139e-5H. Event reciprocity is satisfied to floating precision.
These are equilibrium diagnostics, not rigorous bounds on the nonequilibrium
history or on unknown independent scalar couplings. These kernels are
audited but not included in the evolving leading-portal-order network.

6. Reproduction and review

reaction_rates.py regenerates the invariant rate table. build_decay_bank.py
regenerates conductance banks using an explicitly selected scattering
bank's eta and y grids. decay_graph.py exposes the graph API and a single-T
CLI. scalar_exchange_audit.py regenerates the diagnostic self-channel rates.
The independent method reviewer checked the spin factors, thermal prefactor,
identical-particle bookkeeping, three-body phase space, exact daughter-angle
probabilities, graph orientation, detailed balance, and thermal tail bound.
This is an internal computational/mathematical check, not external peer review.
