An expansion-only sensitivity calculation replaced constant g*=g*s=60 with the published 2+1-flavor lattice-QCD equation of state plus ideal photons, charged leptons and neutrinos. Entropy conservation changes both the temperature-time relation and momentum redshift: a^3 s stays constant, while p/T no longer does. A conservative remap accounts for that transport. With the same frozen ideal collision kernels and initial preparation, final Tkin/T changes from 0.75027 to 0.72220; a separate ideal charm/bottom addition gives 0.70567. Momentum-grid changes shift these results by about 0.0006-0.0007, below the background-induced differences. The constant-60 limit reproduces the frozen result exactly. These central-EOS variations do not propagate lattice covariance, supply interacting-QCD collision rates, establish an earlier cosmology, or predict a physical relic abundance; the two choices are not uncertainty bounds.
